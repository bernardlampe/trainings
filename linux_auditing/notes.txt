* Driver auditing section
    DAC and MAC (SELinux) and how each is implemented
    identify what is available in /dev/ and /proc/ and to whom
    identify fops for each available thing
    focus on things implemented by vendors or third parties (lower-hanging fruit)
    make sure you understand what open and release are doing and are matched up
    focus on mmap and its parameter checks
    focus on extensible drivers such as ion
    focus on things in the driver which are overridden at a higher level, such as the /proc level
